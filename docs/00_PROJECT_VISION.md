# Project vision

## OneInFlow

**Everything in one flow.**

OneInFlow is an **AI-first Customer Operations Platform** for managing customer
conversations and the operational work that follows them through AI and human
collaboration.

The product flow is:

```text
Conversation -> Understanding -> Knowledge -> Action -> Human -> Payment -> Operations
```

The current product is an MVP. It is not positioned as a replacement for every
CRM, a sales-pipeline system, or a fully autonomous support organization.

## Product direction

- Bring customer conversations and follow-up operations into one workspace.
- Ground AI answers in workspace-owned knowledge.
- Convert confirmed customer intent into validated operational actions.
- Let staff take over, assign, read, and resolve attention safely.
- Keep customer payments distinct from OneInFlow subscription billing.
